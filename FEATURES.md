# ContractLens AI — Complete Feature Inventory

This edition retains the full application feature set and applies the premium visual refresh across the existing views.

## 1. Contract intake and analysis
- Upload or drag-and-drop PDF, DOCX, TXT, Markdown, and RTF files; paste contract text directly.
- Contract type auto-detection plus Rental, Loan, and Job/Gig-Work selection.
- Perspective-aware review for tenant, gig worker, employee, borrower, employer, lender, and other signer.
- Jurisdiction selection for India, US general, California, New York, and UK/EU.
- Contract title, file details, word count, analysis pipeline progress, and instant sample agreements.
- Built-in local analysis engine; optional hybrid Gemini/OpenAI provider configuration.

## 2. Smart risk dashboard
- Overall contract safety score, letter grade, verdict, and executive summary.
- Traffic-light clause classification (red / yellow / green).
- Categorized findings, severity filters, clause/page references, source excerpts, and action recommendations.
- Estimated financial exposure, potential extra costs, penalties, and risk category summaries.
- Jurisdiction-aware legality / restriction audit indicators.
- Multiple explanation depths: legal, simple, beginner, and detailed.

## 3. Interactive smart document reader
- Paginated contract reading view with risk highlights linked to findings.
- Select text to open **Explain with AI** or **Define Term** actions.
- Click findings to jump to the matching document passage and vice versa.
- Traffic-light plain-English section view.
- Auto-redline workspace with before/after comparison, projected score change, fix-all action, copy-clean, and `.txt` download.
- Print/export report controls.

## 4. Contract intelligence and risk mapping
- Contract Risk DNA / category-level risk profile.
- Combined-clause risk detection for risks that emerge when provisions are read together.
- Missing-information and incomplete-term detection.
- Interactive clause relationship map with linked clause navigation.
- **Before You Sign** readiness score and question checklist.

## 5. Ask My Contract
- Conversational Q&A grounded in the active agreement.
- Suggested starter questions.
- Answers with section/page citations that jump back to the relevant clause.

## 6. What-if and financial simulations
- Custom hypothetical scenario input.
- Step-by-step scenario flow based on contract terms.
- Preconfigured life-event scenarios such as early exit, missed payments, and account suspension.
- Financial stress test comparing the active contract with a fairer baseline and showing estimated risk avoided.

## 7. Negotiation toolkit
- Interactive AI negotiation sparring arena.
- Select a clause and opponent difficulty (reasonable, tough, hardball).
- Negotiation leverage score, coaching tips, suggested moves, and concessions tracking.
- One-click negotiation email generation in polite, professional, or firm tone.
- Edit recipient/sender names, copy email, and download `.txt`.
- Questions to ask before signing.

## 8. Compare and track versions
- Side-by-side analysis of Contract A and Contract B.
- Structured comparison of financial and legal changes.
- Separate Contract A and Contract B file uploads with drag-and-drop and file-picker support.
- PDF, DOCX, TXT, Markdown, and RTF extraction (up to 8 MB per file), with extracted text loaded into editable comparison fields.
- Paste-text fallback and built-in rental / before-and-after redline demo scenarios.
- Version history tracker for v1 → v2 → v3, including changes to risk and key terms.

## 9. Deadline and renewal centre
- Extract notice periods, due dates, renewal cutoffs, and other date-based obligations.
- Active-contract extracted reminders and persistent reminder vault.
- In-app deadline alert demo.
- Export calendar events as `.ics` for Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, and other calendar applications.

## 10. Legal terms translator
- Searchable legal terminology glossary.
- Quick-pick terms and detailed term view.
- Plain-English descriptions and context for how a term affects the active contract.
- Beginner / ELI5 mode.
- Explicit theme-aware text colors for glossary cards, plain-English definitions, contract excerpts, and signing questions in dark and light modes.
- Staggered glossary-card entrance, animated detail-panel reveal, smoother hover/focus feedback, and reduced-motion accessibility support.

## 11. Voice and multilingual access
- Voice briefing playback with browser speech synthesis.
- Voice question interaction where browser speech recognition is supported.
- Eight language options: English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Spanish, and French.
- Voice selection and speech-rate controls.
- Localized briefing generation for the active agreement.

## 12. Contract vault and memory
- Save and revisit previous contract analyses.
- Summary statistics for audits, risks, reminders, and average safety score.
- Search/query Contract Memory across saved agreements (for recurring renewals, notice periods, penalties, and similar questions).
- Delete individual saved records and clear all local session data.

## 13. Browser extension and partner integrations
- Chrome/Edge Manifest V3 browser assistant.
- Highlight and explain contract clauses on web pages without manual copy/paste.
- Full-page scan workflow.
- In-app ecosystem lab with browser-site walkthroughs.
- Partner-app **Understand This** widget demonstrations for gig, rental, and loan flows.
- Embeddable JavaScript partner widget / SDK and copyable integration snippet.

## 14. Quality-of-life and visual features
- Premium refreshed typography and visual design.
- Responsive desktop/tablet/mobile layout.
- Light and dark themes.
- Toast feedback, sample contract demos, quick links to major modules, and loading progress animation.
- Clipboard helpers, document/email exports, print-friendly report view, and local-first saved data.

## Engine and accuracy notes

The built-in engine uses local contract-analysis logic and heuristics; it is not a guaranteed legal interpretation. Optional AI provider support can add model-assisted analysis when configured. Speech recognition, browser extension behavior, and external fonts depend on browser permissions and platform support. Never rely on an automated score alone for a legal or financial decision.
